# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Nova Era API community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and accept feedback in good faith.
- Focus on what is best for the community and its users.
- Respect privacy and do not share credentials, private conversations or customer data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, personal attacks, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, publishing private information, or any conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional community are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately through the security contact process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), marking the report as a conduct concern. Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban participants who violate this Code.
